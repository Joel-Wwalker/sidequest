# Catalog method

The catalog has 506 entries: the original 19 guides, 300 medication entries and 187 specialty entries. Two original guides cover substance families (anabolic steroids and synthetic cathinones); the other 504 name specific substances or established mixtures. Brands and common salts are searchable aliases rather than additional records. Individual members of a family have their own entries where included.

Each expanded record starts with a credible use context. Its scene then selects an ordinary activity, held object, exposure representation, possible response and downside. The setting is an illustration of that context, not a claim about every person who uses the substance. Medication scenes identify a treatment context; historical and experimental material can use a research or discussion scene.

Sources include MedlinePlus, DailyMed and FDA product information, NIH Office of Dietary Supplements fact sheets, NCCIH summaries, WHO and government drug reports, and primary research. Links live with each entry. Sources may describe a particular formulation, indication, population or study model; the summary and scene must retain those qualifications. An animal or laboratory result does not establish a human benefit.

Full effects and risks have no scene-caption character limit. Short scene notes are written separately, and must preserve uncertainty and the selected route. Do not shorten medical copy by deleting words, qualifiers, conjunctions or the beginning of a sentence. The displayed risks are selected examples, not an exhaustive product label or interaction checker.

Evidence labels describe broad confidence in the selected summary. They do not mean every advertised use is established. Risk is context dependent, and experimental risk can be uncertain; the expansion does not assign invented numerical safety scores. No entry supplies a dose or a treatment recommendation. The site has not undergone clinician review.

Shared animation components keep object attachments and transitions consistent. Each entry must differ in rendered setting, task, prop, mechanism or downside choreography; color, text, speed and unused metadata do not count. The tests collapse shared drawing branches when checking duplicates. Independent Sol reviewers inspect source and rendered evidence using the [scene review procedure](scene-review-procedure.md). Research can use a lower-cost agent, but review remains separate from authorship.
